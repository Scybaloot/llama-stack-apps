MEASURING

  1. Stand about six feet back, waist up in frame, then hold still for the 3-second calibration.
  2. Film yourself and the screen together on a phone at 240fps.
  3. Jump ten times. Then press Space ten times, arm at rest.
  4. In the footage, count frames from your wrists crossing the shoulder line to the marker going white. Each frame is 4.2 ms.
  5. The keypress runs measure display latency alone. Gesture median minus keypress median is the pose pipeline's true cost.
